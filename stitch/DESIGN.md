## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, high-end culinary narrative where the architectural warmth of Kerala converges with the palatial grandeur of Arabian hospitality. The digital experience acts as a dimly lit, richly textured portal—reminiscent of hand-carved teak archways, polished urulis, and glowing brass oil lamps. 

The aesthetic marries **dark cinematic hospitality** with **restrained liquid glass** and **architectural arch motifs**. Rather than utilitarian ecommerce dining patterns, every interaction evokes quiet opulence, sensory immersion, and generous welcome. Subtle specular highlights, delicate gold hairline borders, and glowing amber backdrops ground the user in a tactile, regal environment.

Targeting discerning diners, culinary travelers, and private dining patrons seeking authenticity paired with fine-dining poise, the interface balances historic provenance with modern fluid clarity.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in cinematic darkness, drawn directly from traditional Kerala rosewood interiors, brass artifacts, and royal Mughal architectural tones:

- **Primary (`#49131D` — Deep Castle Burgundy):** Represents royal heritage, richness, and depth. Applied to primary call-to-actions, hero backdrop overlays, and prestigious focal points.
- **Secondary (`#B69A68` — Antique Brass):** Reflects polished Nilavilakku oil lamps and Arabian metalwork. Applied to filigree accents, active navigations, subtle badges, and fine structural dividing lines.
- **Tertiary (`#F7F1E8` — Warm Ivory):** The luminous source of contrast. Used for pristine headlines, primary content labels, and luminous highlights, preventing the harshness of pure digital white.
- **Neutral (`#24201E` — Espresso Charcoal):** Grounding base canvas tone. Deep, smoke-infused charcoal with warm undertones that hosts deep atmospheric shadow falls without turning clinical.

Layering tints include frosted brass rims (`rgba(182, 154, 104, 0.2)`), warm glass panels (`rgba(36, 32, 30, 0.65)`), and amber radiance accents (`rgba(224, 161, 74, 0.15)`).

## Typography

The type system blends the classical editorial gravitas of a high-contrast serif with the crystalline precision of a contemporary geometric sans-serif.

- **Headline Display (EB Garamond):** Conveys regal timelessness, historic literature, and culinary craftsmanship. Generous optical sizes, natural ligatures, and relaxed line heights allow statements like "Every great meal begins with a warm welcome" to breathe as architectural poetry.
- **Body & Functional Hierarchy (DM Sans):** Selected for its neutral clarity, structural legibility against dark surfaces, and balance against serif flourishes.
- **Eyebrow & Subheadings:** Set in strictly spaced uppercase DM Sans with tracking up to `0.22em`, acting as golden filigree markers above major narrative sections.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid orchestrates desktop screens with expansive, asymmetric storytelling zones, tapering into a cohesive 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Atmospheric Rhythms:** Vertical pacing prioritizes generous breathing room (`space-2xl` and `space-xl`) between chapters of the digital space to mirror an unhurried, royal dining cadence.
- **Content Alignment:** Text lockups leverage deliberate split-screen compositions—pairing architectural portals, arch framing, or artisanal culinary imagery on one flank while narrative typography rests on the other.
- **Safe Margins:** Deep outer canvases allow floating floating navigation pills and sticky discovery bars to float effortlessly within the viewport without obscuring primary culinary photography.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through dark-field layering, warmth dissipation, and frosted liquid glass rather than artificial flat drop shadows:

1. **Backdrop Canvas (Ground Level):** Deep Espresso Charcoal (`#24201E`) infused with radial gradients simulating flickering lamplight and distant warm embers.
2. **Restrained Liquid Glass (Mid Level):** Surface containers use semi-translucent fills (`rgba(36, 32, 30, 0.72)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and delicate hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(182, 154, 104, 0.25)`).
3. **Floating Sanctuary Panels (High Level):** Modals, top navigation lozenges, and hero interactive cards lift with subtle golden ambient glows: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 25px 0 rgba(182, 154, 104, 0.08)`.
4. **Specular Edging:** Subtle linear gradients on top component borders create fine metal-catch highlights that echo polished brass finishes.

## Shapes

The geometry draws directly from traditional Kerala temple joinery and Saracenic cusped arches, manifested digitally via elongated capsules and rounded silhouettes:

- **Capsule Contours (Level 3):** Pill-shaped buttons, navbars, and badge pills represent smooth stones and polished metal handles.
- **Architectural Arch Motifs:** Large media frames, featured story cards, and tasting vignettes adopt top parabolic curves (`border-radius: 12rem 12rem 1rem 1rem` on desktop hero visual frames), immediately evoking the majestic entryways of ancient palaces.
- **Internal Card Framing:** Rounded interiors maintain a soft `1rem` to `1.5rem` radius, avoiding brutalist corners and preserving an inviting, tactile softness.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Burgundy Fill:** Pill-shaped (`roundedness: 3`) with a rich Deep Castle Burgundy background (`#49131D`), bordered with an ultra-thin Antique Brass perimeter (`1px solid rgba(182, 154, 104, 0.4)`). Text in Warm Ivory (`#F7F1E8`), accompanied by directional arrows. On hover, a soft internal brass radiance expands (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 16px rgba(182, 154, 104, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Pill:** Transparent backdrop with a hairline brass border (`1px solid #B69A68`), Warm Ivory label, and integrated antique iconography (e.g., cutlery, brass lamps).

### Floating Capsule Navigation
- Suspended top navigation pill constructed with liquid glass (`rgba(36, 32, 30, 0.7)`), 20px blur, and an overarching antique brass hairline border. Includes miniature castled crest iconography, small-caps links, and a self-contained Burgundy CTA pill.

### Menu & Feature Cards
- Dark espresso containers with soft arched photography crowns or full curved headers. Item listings display serif dish titles, gold-leaf-styled price indicators (`#B69A68`), and delicate multi-line provenance descriptions in muted Warm Ivory (`rgba(247, 241, 232, 0.7)`).

### Badges, Emblems & Filigree Dividers
- Section separators incorporate ornamental filigree motifs centered around a slender horizontal brass line. 
- Value pillars (e.g., "Rooted in Kerala", "Inspired by the World") feature delicate line-art iconography etched in antique brass with centered capital labels.

### Form Inputs & Reservation Fields
- Translucent input wells with soft inner depth (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`), surrounded by muted brass borders that illuminate in warm golden amber (`#B69A68`) when focused. Placeholder copy set in low-opacity Warm Ivory.
